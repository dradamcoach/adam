# ADAM - Platform, privacy, security and accessibility requirements (desk research)

Research date: 2026-10-15. Method: official pages opened with WebFetch; summaries are from the fetch tool, so read the quoted guideline numbers yourself before acting. Status key: clear = the source states the rule plainly; needs legal review = judgement about ADAM's facts or law; UNVERIFIED = I could not open an official page that proves it. This is not legal advice.

ADAM facts assumed (from the brief): marketplace of coaches/clinics/clubs/gyms/stores; health and rehabilitation data; minors in sports clubs; in-app store selling physical goods and supplements; user-generated profiles/content; applicant document uploads (identity documents); Firebase backend; website wrapped with Capacitor for iOS and Android; Arabic/English; owner is likely in Egypt.

## Top 10 items the owner must decide or ask a lawyer about
1. Egypt PDPL: does ADAM (as a person or a company) need a PDPC licence for sensitive health data and for cross-border transfer, and is the grace-period end 31 Oct or 1 Nov 2026? Who is the DPO?
2. Legal entity: Apple 5.1.1(ix) says healthcare-type apps must be submitted by a legal entity; decide company structure before creating store accounts.
3. Are Firebase servers outside Egypt acceptable under the transfer rules (consent, licence, region choice)?
4. Minors in sports clubs: allow child accounts at all? Guardian consent flow, age screen, store audience declarations (Apple 5.1.4, Google Families, PDPL, GDPR Art 8).
5. Identity-document uploads: retention period, who can see them, and deletion after verification.
6. Medical claims and device status: which features (assessments, rehab plans, "risk scores") could count as diagnosis or a medical device; approved disclaimer wording in Arabic and English.
7. Supplement and pharma sales: which products are allowed, who vets sellers/ingredients, and the legal basis in Egypt; how this fits Google's unapproved-substances rule and Apple's rules.
8. Payments: which products are physical goods (external payment) versus digital subscriptions or programmes (in-app purchase) and how marketplace commission and clinic bookings are classified.
9. Data retained after account deletion (orders, invoices, safety records) and how deletion is explained in the privacy policy and Data safety form.
10. EU exposure: block EU stores, or accept GDPR Art 9 / EAA obligations (DPO, accessible store and checkout).
